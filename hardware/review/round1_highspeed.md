# Round 1 review: high-speed interfaces and networking

Scope: USB-C SuperSpeed/USB2 data path, RTL8156BG NICs, the PHY-to-PHY links to the RTL8372N, the RTL8372N switch, the RTL8221B PHY with its RJ45, the router-module placeholder, net classes and the two-board split.
Sources: `hardware/gen/blocks/usbc_port.py`, `ethernet.py`, `poe.py`, `aux_power.py`, `mcu.py`, `hardware/gen/project_template.kicad_pro`, `hardware/mechanical/mech_spec.json`, and the datasheets in `docs/datasheets/`.
Method: adversarial review. Every pin connection was checked against the generated netlist (`Design.netlist()`), not only against the source text.

**Datasheet text.** `RTL8372N_datasheet.txt` and `RTL8221B_datasheet_LCSC_C5155988.txt` in `docs/datasheets/text/` were empty. Both PDFs use RC4-128 (R3) encryption with an empty user password, and the pages sit in object streams that `pdftxt.py` cannot read. I decrypted them and flattened the object streams, then re-extracted both datasheets plus `TPD4E05U06_datasheet.txt`, which was missing. I wrote the results to `docs/datasheets/text/` (the Realtek watermark lines are removed). The Realtek tables extract with words run together, so search for joined strings such as `STR_EN_PHY` or `SEL_XTAL`.

Severity scale: **CRITICAL** = will not work or causes damage; **MAJOR** = a function is broken or a rule is violated; **MINOR** = improvement or check. Findings that rest on facts not in the datasheets are marked *unverified*, and their severity is lowered.

## Checked and correct (no action)
- **USB-C SuperSpeed mapping, both plug orientations.** The J pins (A2/A3 = TX1, B11/B10 = RX1, B2/B3 = TX2, A11/A10 = RX2) go to HD3SS3212 B0/B1 and C0/C1. A0 is the NIC TX (through the caps) and A1 is the NIC RX. The HD3SS3212 truth table is "SEL L: Port A to Port B, H: Port A to Port C". The TPS26750 TRM GPIO event `Cable_Orientation_Event` is "Low when … upside-up (CC1) … High when … upside-down (CC2)". SEL low selects TX1/RX1 for CC1, so the polarity is correct with the event's polarity bit left at 0. P/N polarity is preserved end to end.
- **USB3 AC coupling.** There is one 100 nF pair, on the device TX only, between the NIC and mux port A. This matches the HD3SS3212 guidance: "the designer must place the capacitors on one side of the switch". The device RX is DC-coupled, which is correct because the host's TX carries those caps.
- **HD3SS3212 RSVD1/RSVD2.** The datasheet says these "can be left not connected or can be fed to VCC", so NC is fine. OEn tied to GND means always enabled.
- **TPD4E05U06 on the SS lines.** 0.5 pF typical (DQA package) and flow-through routing. Fine for 5 Gbps.
- **RTL8372N:** RSET 2.49 kΩ 1 %, RSV_TEST0 pulled down 4.7k, INI_MODE straps at their default 11 (boot from SPI flash), and S0/S1 TX coupling caps of 100 nF (datasheet CTX 80–120 nF).
- **RTL8372N reset timing.** The 10k/2.2 µF RC releases about 20 ms after V3V3_ETH, which is at least the 10.5 ms required.
- **RTL8221B:** RSET 2.49k 1 %, MDIO pull-up 1.5k to 3.3 V, DVDD3318 on 3.3 V (3.3 V MDIO, matching the RTL8372N DVDDIO5 domain), PHYRSTB RC ≥ 10 ms, and SerDes caps in both directions.
- **PHY-to-PHY pair order.** NIC pair i goes to switch pair A..D, straight through. Both ends have auto-crossover and auto polarity correction, and P stays on P.

## Top 5
1. **H-1** Switch SPI flash DI/DO swapped: the RTL8372N cannot boot.
2. **H-2** SEL_XTAL pulled down selects a 50 MHz reference with a 25 MHz crystal: no clocks, no links.
3. **H-3** The two-board split pushes 16 MDI pairs plus power and control through one 40-pin 1.27 mm header, which cannot be built. Move the switch onto board A next to the NICs so only 4 SerDes pairs cross, on a separate differential mezzanine.
4. **H-4 / H-5** Switch straps at their defaults give a slave I2C management interface (the router speaks MDIO) and embedded PHYs disabled. Pull up REG_IF_SEL_1/0 and EN_PHY.
5. **H-7 / H-8** The capacitive 2.5GBASE-T PHY-to-PHY links are undocumented by Realtek and have no common-mode rejection. Separately, the NIC only works when the partner accepts DR_Swap: get Realtek's confirmation and configure TPS26750 "Initiate Swap to UFP".

Also high priority: **H-6** (mux SEL back-powering when ETH is off), **H-9** (RTL8221B MDI-swap strap floating), **H-10** (0.95 V ripple/PWM requirement; 21 V to 0.95 V cannot run above 1 MHz), **H-11 / H-12** (net classes missing for SerDes, RJ45 MDI and USB2; widths below the 2 oz minimum).
