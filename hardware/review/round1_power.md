# Round 1 adversarial review: power path and safety

Scope: USB-C ports (BQ25756 + TPS26750 + TPD4S480), aux inputs (TPS26631 + LM74700 + BQ25756), DC output, PoE PD + flyback, aux rails, battery interface, USB-A / Qi / lighting power parts, power budget.
Sources: `hardware/gen/blocks/*.py` (the schematic), `docs/design-rules.md`, `docs/power-budget.md`, `docs/chip-metadata.md`, datasheet text in `docs/datasheets/text/` (LM5155, SLUAAY7 and SLUAAX9 text extracted for this review).
Severity: CRITICAL = damages hardware, unsafe, or does not work at all; MAJOR = function broken or design rule violated; MINOR = improvement or unverified risk.

**Totals: 6 CRITICAL, 23 MAJOR, 20 MINOR.**

## Checked and found consistent with the datasheets (no finding)

- **BQ25756 orientation and sensing:**
  - HS1 drain on VIN (after ACP→ACN), LS1 on SW1, HS2 drain on VOUT (SRP side), sense VOUT→VBAT (SRP/SRN). The SW1 leg is on the VAC side and the SW2 leg on the battery side, so there is no body-diode path from VBAT to VBUS when idle.
  - ACP-ACN 470 nF + ACP-GND 100 nF and SRP-SRN 470 nF + SRP-GND 100 nF match the pin table.
- **BQ25756 programming values:**
  - V_FB 1.536 V; R_TOP 249 kΩ is TI's value.
  - K_ICHG = 50 A·kΩ at 5 mΩ, so 7.5 kΩ = 6.67 A.
  - K_ILIM = 50 A·kΩ at 2 mΩ becomes 20 A·kΩ at 5 mΩ, so 4.02 kΩ = 4.98 A (consistent with the IAC_DPM register scaling).
  - R_FSW 80.6 kΩ ≈ 347 kHz (Table 6-2).
  - TS 5.23 kΩ / 30.1 kΩ matches RT1 5.24 kΩ / RT2 30.31 kΩ; the NTC is B25/85 = 3434 K, which matches 103AT.
- **BQ25756 pin handling:**
  - DRV_SUP tied to REGN is allowed.
  - "ACUV to VAC, ACOV to PGND" is the datasheet's unused option (but see P-8).
  - NC pins 15, 16 and 31 are left floating as required.
- **TPS26750:**
  - ADCIN straps: port 1 (LDO_3V3, GND) = (7,0); port 2 (GND, GND) = (0,0); port 3, 24 kΩ / 100 kΩ = 0.806, giving (6,0); port 4 (LDO_1V5, LDO_3V3) = (5,7). All are SafeMode #1 to #4 per Tables 7-2 and 7-6.
  - CC caps 330 pF are within 200 to 480 pF.
  - LDO_1V5 10 µF is within 4.5 to 12 µF.
  - POWER_PATH_EN floating is allowed when unused (but see P-2).
- **TPD4S480:**
  - RPD_Gx shorted to C_CCx (dead-battery Rd).
  - VBIAS 100 nF/100 V.
  - EPR_EN driven by GPIO1 with a 100 kΩ pull-down.
- **LM74700:** EN tied to ANODE (always on), VCAP 100 nF to ANODE, FET drain on CATHODE and source on ANODE.
- **TPS26631:**
  - Blocking FET source on IN_SYS, drain on IN, AO3400A across B_GATE/DRV/IN_SYS, per Fig 8-7.
  - ILIM 11 kΩ = 1.65 A (18.18/R).
  - MODE = GND is auto-retry.
  - UV 10.0 V.
- **TPS2373-4:**
  - DEN 24.9 kΩ, REF 49.9 kΩ.
  - Class 6 = CLSA 63.4 Ω (class 4) + CLSB 249 Ω (class 1), as in TIDA-050023.
  - APD tied to RTN (unused).
  - Two 4-diode bridges (SS310, 100 V / 57 V = 1.75×).
  - SMAJ58A.
- **Aux bucks:**
  - TPS62933 FB dividers: 3.33 V / 4.98 V / 12.0 V / 0.950 V.
  - Inductors per Table 10-2.
  - NIC buck on-time at 21 V to 0.95 V and 310 kHz is 146 ns, above the 70 ns minimum.
