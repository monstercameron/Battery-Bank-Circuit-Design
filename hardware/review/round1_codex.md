**Connections verified in the reviewed revision.** All six BQ stages have the expected high-side drain and low-side source orientation. ACP/ACN straddle their input shunts, and SRP/SRN straddle the converter-to-VBAT shunts in the expected forward-charge direction. The reversed shunt in X-1 is the separate **cartridge** shunt. The LM74700 ORing MOSFETs also have source at the input and drain at the common output.

For each USB port, HD3SS3212 `SEL=0` routes NIC TX through its 100 nF capacitors to **A2/A3** and RX from **B11/B10**. `SEL=1` routes TX to **B2/B3** and RX from **A11/A10**. USB2 D+/D− use the TPD4S480 SBU protection channels as permitted by TI. S1 TX/RX are crossed correctly to the external PHY. In this revision switch pin 6 `SPF_SDI` drives flash DI, and pin 8 `SPF_SDO` receives flash DO. The 25 MHz clock selection, embedded-PHY enable and MDIO-management straps are consistent with the intended switch configuration. These tracing results leave the PD image, physical channel and network initialization as the open issues. [HD3SS3212 switching table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/hd3ss3212.pdf) · [TPD4S480 application information](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd4s480.pdf)

The main I²C map is coherent with one mux channel selected at a time: PD controllers `0x20–0x23`, expanders `0x26/0x27`, RTC `0x51`, mux `0x70`, six chargers at `0x6B` on channels 0–5, and NFC/gauge at `0x55` on channels 6/7. The private PD EEPROMs can each use `0x50`. GPIO0, 2, 5 and 12 have boot-compatible biases. Holding the flashlight button changes the GPIO15 reset strap and can suppress ROM logging. The revised always-on TPS62933 EN pins float as TI permits. The revised NIC/PHY buck’s nominal 0.95 V from 5 V at 1.2 MHz requires about **158 ns** on-time against TI’s 70 ns typical. The schematic review did not establish measured rail ripple, USB eyes, BER, thermal margins, interrupt capacity or production stock. **The five changes I would make first**

1. **Correct and validate the cartridge on a cell simulator.** Fix both sense pairs, the gauge divider, protector configuration and communication reference. Demonstrate that each fault opens the intended FET before connecting real cells.
2. **Separate pack charging from system-bus regulation.** Give one charger authority over actual cell current and temperature, add independent charge/discharge limits, and implement controlled cartridge precharge and disconnect. Recalculate the battery-only guarantee from that architecture.
3. **Redesign and prove one complete USB-C port before replicating it four times.** Include connector isolation, discharge, dead-battery bootstrap, bounded pre-contract draw and effective BQ bulk. Demonstrate 100 W SPR sourcing, source/UFP enumeration, detach and hard reset with a PD analyzer.
4. **Replace the placeholder PoE flyback with a qualified design.** Select the exact MagJack, transformer and insulation components. Resolve startup, granted-class limiting, switch/shunt sizing and snubber energy on a standalone prototype.
5. **Freeze the router module and board partition before layout.** Draw every mezzanine contact, qualify one complete NIC-to-switch link, and make the release gate require correct exported pin connectivity, passing ERC, per-board BOM/CPL and resolved critical-part availability.
