# Round 1 adversarial review: control, radios, audio/lighting, JLCPCB DFM, debuggability

Reviewer scope: `hardware/gen/blocks/{mcu,lora,audio,lighting,usb_a,qi}.py`, the I2C/IRQ/enable wiring in `usbc_port.py`, `power_input.py`, `dc_output.py`, `aux_power.py`, `ethernet.py`, the generator (`bb.py`, `kicad_writer.py`, `build.py`), `checks/check.py`, `kicad/bom_jlcpcb.csv`, `mechanical/mech_spec.json`.

Snapshot note: `mcu.py`, `battery.py` and a new `cartridge.py` were modified at 10:14-10:15 while this review was running (TCA9555 P13 became `EXP_P13_SPARE`; the BQ34Z100 moved to a separate cartridge project). Findings refer to the sources as read at 10:15. JLCPCB stock/class data from `hardware/tools/jlc.py --code` on 2026-11-21.

Severity: CRITICAL = board will not work, will be damaged or cannot be ordered; MAJOR = function/safety/rule violation that must be fixed before layout; MINOR = improvement, risk or unverified item. Items marked **VERIFY** are not confirmed from a datasheet in `docs/datasheets/` and have had their severity lowered.

## Items checked and found OK

- **ESP32 strapping pins:** IO0 has a 10k pull-up plus BOOT. IO2 has a 100k pull-down (RED_LED gate). IO12 has a 100k pull-down plus the amp SD_MODE path (low at boot → 3.3 V flash). IO15 has a 10k pull-up (FLASH button; pressing it during reset only mutes the ROM log). IO5 has a 10k pull-up (SD_CS).
- **ESP32 inputs and resources:** input-only IO34/35/36/39 are used only as inputs with external pull-ups. Flash pins 17-22 are NC on the module. UART0 is reserved for the programming header. EN RC is 10k/1 µF. 3V3 bulk is 22 µF + 1 µF + 100 nF. I2S0 PDM RX (IO32 clock / IO33 data, two mics with SELECT = GND/V3V3) plus I2S1 TX to the amp is a supported ESP32 combination. The U.FL module choice suits the metal case.
- **UART crossover:** nRF P0.06 (`LORA_TX`) goes to ESP32 IO16 (U2RXD default), and ESP32 IO17 (U2TXD) goes to nRF P0.08. The E22 has a single NRST pad (pin 15) in the symbol, so it is not duplicated. DIO2 → TXEN and RXEN come from the MCU as intended.
- **I2C address map:** no conflicts. TPS26750 0x20-0x23 is confirmed against TPS26750 Table 7-5. TCA9555 is at 0x27 (A2..A0 = 111), PCF85063A at 0x51, TCA9548A at 0x70. BQ25756 0x6B appears once per mux channel. NT3H2111 0x55 (ch6) and the cartridge BQ34Z100 0x55 (ch7) are on separate channels; firmware must never enable ch6 and ch7 together. All I2C devices are on always-on V3V3, so no I2C back-powering was found.
- **TPS62933 EN on gated rails:** 0.7 µA × 100k = 70 mV is below the 1.1 V threshold. This would be correct if the TCA9535 were used (C-1).
- **BOM CSV columns:** Comment / Designator / Footprint / LCSC Part # (+Qty) match JLC's template. DNP and `_nobom` parts are excluded. Test pads stay off the BOM.
