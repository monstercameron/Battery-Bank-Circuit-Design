### Mechanical spec rev C: open inconsistencies found by the 3D build
These were left for the lead to fix in `hardware/mechanical/mech_spec.json`, then run `sync_mech_spec.py`.
1. **No room for the LED optic.** The LED board sits at x −4 (the outer face), leaving 0 mm for the 20 mm TIR optic. Actually lengthen the left cap to 16 mm (both `end_caps.left.outer_length` and `outer_to_interior_offset.x`), or move the LED board inward.
2. **Right end cap length is missing.** The spec implies 4 mm; state it.
3. **DC jacks hit the 2×24 header.** The jack bodies (about 14 mm deep) run into the header at y 74–79. The "DC jacks" zone is only 8.5 mm deep.
4. **SerDes mezzanine overlaps the 2×24 header** and falls outside the B2B strip. Give it real dimensions, and define whether x/y is the left end or the centre.
5. **Board A left screw boss lands on `J_LEDHARNESS`** (both at y 44.5) and intrudes into the USB-C zone.
6. **Fan footprint ends at x 138.5, but the keep-out ends at 134.** The fan centre is at z 47 versus the grille at z 46.
7. **Board A zones:**
   - "Input + DC-out" overlaps "Aux/USB-A/ESP32/microSD" by 18×6 mm.
   - The Input zone (42×20) can't fit two BQ25756 stages.
   - The DC-jack zone can't fit its TVS and MLCCs.
8. **Board B zones:**
   - The router zone (43×42) can't fit an SM81 (50×36).
   - The PoE flyback transformer (about 14×12×10 mm) is under the speaker's 3.5 mm height keepout.
9. **Height-keepout coordinates are ambiguous** (interior frame vs board-local). They leave a 0.5 mm gap under the speaker.
10. **Card-guide rails need notches at all edge connectors.** The model already draws them; record them in the spec.
11. **RJ45 cutout reaches the corner radius** (z 62.1 vs a flat face ending at 62). The glow note should say z 4..62.
12. **Smaller items:**
    - Board B's screw boss has no position.
    - The tripod insert sits over the cartridge, with no depth for its boss.
    - The right boss clears the cartridge by only 1 mm.

The VSYS architecture (an extra BQ stage plus a hot-swap path) adds board area, so redo the board A zone plan (items 7 and 8) together with step 4 below.

### Lead-owned tooling changes made just before the pause
These are **compiled but not yet exercised by a full build.**
- **`hardware/gen/build.py`**
  - **X-36 staged builds:** builds go to `<out>.staging` and are promoted only when the checker **and** KiCad ERC both pass. `--only` builds default to `hardware/kicad_partial/`.
  - **X-36 shared library:** `bb.kicad_sym` is now a merged union of symbols, written atomically.
  - **X-35 ERC gating:** KiCad ERC errors fail the build; `kicad_outputs()` returns the error and warning counts. A missing kicad-cli is an error unless `BB_SKIP_ERC=1`.
- **`hardware/gen/kicad_writer.py`**
  - **X-41 resolver rules:** `Part.resolve()` lets pin-number keys win, rejects conflicting assignments and dead name keys, and rejects NC on a connected pin.
  - **X-41 label IDs:** label UUIDs are seeded per project/sheet/ref/pin.
- **`hardware/checks/check.py`**
  - **X-34 resistor power:** resistors with no `Op_P` get a computed worst-case V²/R check (`3TO1R`).
  - **X-34 unknown stress:** resistors between equal non-zero rails are reported as INFO (stress undetermined).
- **`.gitignore`:** ignores `hardware/*.staging/` and `hardware/kicad_partial/`.

**Expect the first full build after these changes to surface new errors.** Fix the design (or set a justified `Op_P`), not the checker. Known cases:
- Cartridge ERC had 3 errors on U2 TLV70433.
- `3TO1R` will flag low-value resistors from high rails, e.g. PoE R141/R144.
- The resolver may now reject pin-key conflicts that used to pass silently.
